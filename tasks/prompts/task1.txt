Bugfix: Table Ratio Calculation and Flexible Column Collapse

Problem Description:
When rendering tables with a mix of fixed-width columns and flexible ratio columns in constrained console widths (e.g. nested tables, or dashboards with Console(width=60) and expand=True), flexible ratio columns collapse to zero width, producing collapsed borders '||' with completely vanished column headers and cell values. 

Additionally, rendering empty tables with expand=True in constrained widths or with zero ratios crashes with unhandled AssertionErrors ("Sum of ratios must be > 0") or ZeroDivisionErrors during ratio calculation.

Reproduction:
```python
console = Console(width=60, record=True)
table = Table(title="Worker Dashboard", expand=True)
table.add_column("Service", width=22)
table.add_column("Host", width=18)
table.add_column("Load", ratio=1)
table.add_row("Database Primary", "db-node-01.internal", "82%")

console.print(table)
rendered = console.export_text()
# Bug: 'Load' header and '82%' cell vanish completely into collapsed borders '||'
```

Requirements:
1. Flexible ratio columns must maintain a viable minimum width (at least 1 character for content plus column padding) rather than collapsing to invisible 0-width columns with vanished headers and data.
2. Ratio calculations and space distributions must gracefully handle edge cases (zero available space, negative remaining space, or all-zero ratios) without crashing with unhandled AssertionErrors or ZeroDivisionErrors.
3. Existing unit tests must continue to pass without regressions.
